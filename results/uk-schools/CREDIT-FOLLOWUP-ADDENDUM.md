## Why

school-stock found that 333 of its 667 placeholder pages carry the template's closing sentence
("... values diversity and inclusion and is committed to providing an accessible online
experience") but no Juniper Websites credit, and that 327 of those 333 keep exactly three fields
of GOV.UK's sample statement ("[email address]", "[phone number]", "[number]"). After the result
came in, one of these pages was opened once (2026-11-09); it credits "Website design by Greenhouse
School Websites". One page is not a count. This addendum counts that credit on all of them, and
makes the follow-up's K1 (90% carry a Juniper-family credit) unlikely to hold, which is recorded
here before the re-read rather than after.

## Added credits (searched in the whole HTML, case-insensitive, like the others)

- `greenhouse`: `greenhouseschoolwebsites` written as one word (its web address), or the words
  "Greenhouse School Websites" with any spaces, `&nbsp;` or `&#160;` between them.
- `finalsite`: the word `finalsite` (the US school-website company whose 2017 template
  district-stock counted; 30 of school-stock's 667 keep a placeholder of that template).

Each is stored per host as a flag next to the four registered ones.

## Added prediction

- L1: of the graded set's hosts whose school-stock row has the closing sentence
  (`juniper_template` true), no Juniper credit (`juniper_credit` false) and placeholder ids exactly
  `gov-uk/email_address;gov-uk/phone;gov-uk/number_of_days`, at least 80% carry a `greenhouse`
  credit. Graded only with at least 50 such hosts.

L1 was written after one of these pages had been seen, so it is weaker evidence than K1 to K3. It
is graded the same way and published whether it holds or not.
