# title2-date-slips part 11: where did "April 24, 2027" come from? (pre-registration)

Registered 2026-11-14 at about 17:25 UTC, before any search for this part was run. Made by MV2 of
Munim, Inc. with AI agents; not yet checked by a person. Text CC BY 4.0.

## Known before registering

- The Justice Department's interim final rule (AG Order 6742-2026, 91 FR 20912, dated April 20, 2026)
  moved the first ADA Title II web compliance date from April 24, 2026 to April 26, 2027 (large
  entities) and the second from April 26, 2027 to April 26, 2028 (28 CFR 35.200(b)).
- Parts 1 to 10 of title2-date-slips found pages giving "April 24, 2027" instead of April 26, 2027 at
  universities, county associations and state agencies. In part 10, the Delaware GIC accessibility
  newsletter dated April 17, 2026 gave April 24, 2027, and an Oregon agency page said it was
  "notified on April 17" of the change. Nothing else about April 17 is known; no DOJ press page or
  OIRA record for April 14 to 20 has been read for this part.

## Questions and predictions (fixed now)

- O1: at least one public page dated April 14 to 19, 2026 states a new (2027) first Title II web
  date, i.e. the change was public before the rule's April 20 date.
- O2: among candidate pages dated April 14 to 30, 2026 that state a 2027 first date, the share giving
  April 24, 2027 is higher for pages dated April 14 to 19 than for pages dated April 20 to 30.
  Graded only if each side has at least 3 pages; otherwise reported, not graded.
- O3: the Justice Department's own announcement pages (justice.gov, ada.gov) give April 26, 2027 and
  never April 24, 2027 (the slip did not start at DOJ).

## Method

- Fixed queries, web-wide (no domain filter) unless marked, run once each in this order:
  1. `"April 24, 2027" Title II ADA web accessibility deadline extended`
  2. `"April 26, 2027" Title II ADA deadline extended April 2026`
  3. `DOJ extends ADA Title II web accessibility compliance date April 2026 press release`
  4. `"April 24, 2027" "Title II" April 17 2026`
  5. `justice.gov ADA Title II interim final rule compliance date extension 2026` (allowed domains
     justice.gov, ada.gov)
  6. `OIRA interim final rule ADA Title II web accessibility compliance dates April 2026`
  7. `"April 24, 2027" ADA newsletter April 2026`
  8. `"April 26, 2027" ADA Title II "April 17"`
- A candidate is any result that, by its title or the search summary, could be dated April 14 to 30,
  2026 and could state a Title II date. Every candidate is read with WebFetch (quotes asked for the
  page's date and every Title II date). A page that cannot be read (403, 404, PDF over the reader's
  limit) is listed as unread and not counted. The search summary alone is never used for a count.
- Per page, recorded: URL, publisher type (federal, state or local government, university,
  association, vendor or law firm, news, other), date shown on the page (publication or update; if
  none, "undated" and not counted for O1/O2), and first-date level: April 24, 2027 / April 26, 2027 /
  another 2027 day / no 2027 day.
- Repeated copies of one item (syndication, archive excerpts) count once, at the earliest date shown.

## Limits

A web search reaches only what its index holds and ranks; pages changed since April may show new
text under an old date. This is a small hand check, not a census, and it reads what pages say.
